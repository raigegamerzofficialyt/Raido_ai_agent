# Prospect scoring rubric (0-100)

| Criterion | Points | How to judge (from public evidence only) |
|-----------|-------:|------------------------------------------|
| Posts video regularly | 0-25 | Recent uploads in the last 30-60 days |
| Clear editing need | 0-25 | Raw/unpolished cuts, no captions, no Shorts/Reels despite long videos |
| Business viability | 0-20 | Real product/service, professional presence, an audience |
| Reachable legitimately | 0-15 | Public business contact page/form exists (15), only social DM (8), none (0) |
| Fit with user's skills/niche | 0-15 | Matches services the user offers |

Rules: score only what you observed; write the evidence in `score_reason`. Under 40 = `skipped` unless the user disagrees. 70+ = `qualified`.
Red flags -> skip: scam-looking sites, "get rich" schemes, adult/illegal content, anyone who asks for payment upfront from the editor.
